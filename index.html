❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know Before Ordering

1. Do you ship saplings outside Andhra Pradesh? ▼
Yes! We ship wholesale bulk orders and retail plants across all states in India (Telangana, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Kerala, MP, Odisha, etc.) using safe truck freight with ventilated packaging.
2. Are your fruit plants 100% grafted? ▼
Over 95% of our fruit saplings (Mango, Jackfruit, Guava, Sapota, Citrus) are 100% grafted using high-yielding mother tree budwood for fast fruiting within 1 to 2 years.
3. How do I grow High-Chill Blueberry plants in warm climates? ▼
Our Biloxi and Emerald blueberry varieties are acclimatized low/medium-chill varieties that thrive in temperature ranges of 15°C to 38°C. We provide soil preparation guides (pH 4.5 - 5.5 acidic mix) with every order.
